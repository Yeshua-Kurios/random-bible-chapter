# Proverbs 12 (KJV27)

Drafters: hand-polished by Claude, from the free-model draft

| v | KJV | KJV27 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Whoso loveth instruction loveth knowledge: but he that hateth reproof [is] brutish. | Whoever loves discipline loves knowledge, but whoever hates correction is stupid. _(correction: "brutish" became "stupid" (Hebrew baar: dull, like a beast); "instruction" became "discipline" (Hebrew musar).)_ |
| 2 | A good [man] obtaineth favour of the LORD: but a man of wicked devices will he condemn. | The good obtain favor from the LORD, but he condemns those who devise wickedness. |
| 3 | A man shall not be established by wickedness: but the root of the righteous shall not be moved. | No one is established by wickedness, but the root of the righteous will never be moved. |
| 4 | A virtuous woman [is] a crown to her husband: but she that maketh ashamed [is] as rottenness in his bones. | A wife of noble character is her husband's crown, but a disgraceful wife is like rot in his bones. _(correction: "virtuous woman" became "wife of noble character" (Hebrew eshet chayil: a woman of worth, here a wife); the second line also reads "wife" for parallelism.)_ |
| 5 | The thoughts of the righteous [are] right: [but] the counsels of the wicked [are] deceit. | The thoughts of the righteous are just; the counsels of the wicked are deceit. |
| 6 | The words of the wicked [are] to lie in wait for blood: but the mouth of the upright shall deliver them. | The words of the wicked lie in wait for blood, but the speech of the upright rescues them. |
| 7 | The wicked are overthrown, and [are] not: but the house of the righteous shall stand. | The wicked are overthrown and are no more, but the house of the righteous will stand. |
| 8 | A man shall be commended according to his wisdom: but he that is of a perverse heart shall be despised. | A person is commended according to their good sense, but one with a twisted mind is despised. |
| 9 | [He that is] despised, and hath a servant, [is] better than he that honoureth himself, and lacketh bread. | Better to be lightly esteemed and have a servant than to play the great man and lack bread. _(correction: KJV "despised ... honoureth himself" reads backwards for modern readers; the sense is lowly but provided for, versus pretending to be important.)_ |
| 10 | A righteous [man] regardeth the life of his beast: but the tender mercies of the wicked [are] cruel. | The righteous care for the needs of their animals, but the mercies of the wicked are cruel. |
| 11 | He that tilleth his land shall be satisfied with bread: but he that followeth vain [persons is] void of understanding. | Whoever works his land will have plenty of bread, but whoever chases empty pursuits lacks sense. |
| 12 | The wicked desireth the net of evil [men]: but the root of the righteous yieldeth [fruit]. | The wicked desire the net of evildoers, but the root of the righteous yields fruit. _(correction: The Hebrew of the first line is uncertain ("net" is literal; some read "plunder"). Flagged for review.)_ |
| 13 | The wicked is snared by the transgression of [his] lips: but the just shall come out of trouble. | An evil person is trapped by the sin of his lips, but the righteous escape from trouble. |
| 14 | A man shall be satisfied with good by the fruit of [his] mouth: and the recompence of a man's hands shall be rendered unto him. | From the fruit of their words people are filled with good, and the work of their hands returns to them. |
| 15 | The way of a fool [is] right in his own eyes: but he that hearkeneth unto counsel [is] wise. | The way of a fool is right in his own eyes, but a wise person listens to advice. |
| 16 | A fool's wrath is presently known: but a prudent [man] covereth shame. | A fool's anger is known at once, but the prudent overlook an insult. _(correction: "covereth shame" became "overlook an insult" (Hebrew: conceals dishonor done to him); "presently" (immediately in the KJV) became "at once".)_ |
| 17 | [He that] speaketh truth sheweth forth righteousness: but a false witness deceit. | Whoever speaks the truth gives honest testimony, but a false witness utters deceit. |
| 18 | There is that speaketh like the piercings of a sword: but the tongue of the wise [is] health. | Rash words pierce like swords, but the tongue of the wise brings healing. |
| 19 | The lip of truth shall be established for ever: but a lying tongue [is] but for a moment. | Truthful lips endure forever, but a lying tongue lasts only a moment. |
| 20 | Deceit [is] in the heart of them that imagine evil: but to the counsellors of peace [is] joy. | Deceit is in the heart of those who plot evil, but those who counsel peace have joy. |
| 21 | There shall no evil happen to the just: but the wicked shall be filled with mischief. | No harm comes to the righteous, but the wicked are filled with trouble. |
| 22 | Lying lips [are] abomination to the LORD: but they that deal truly [are] his delight. | Lying lips are an abomination to the LORD, but those who act faithfully are his delight. |
| 23 | A prudent man concealeth knowledge: but the heart of fools proclaimeth foolishness. | A prudent person conceals knowledge, but the heart of fools proclaims folly. |
| 24 | The hand of the diligent shall bear rule: but the slothful shall be under tribute. | The hand of the diligent will rule, but the lazy will be put to forced labor. |
| 25 | Heaviness in the heart of man maketh it stoop: but a good word maketh it glad. | Anxiety in a person's heart weighs it down, but a good word cheers it up. |
| 26 | The righteous [is] more excellent than his neighbour: but the way of the wicked seduceth them. | The righteous choose their friends carefully, but the way of the wicked leads them astray. _(correction: KJV "more excellent than his neighbour" is one reading of an uncertain Hebrew phrase; this follows the reading "guides/searches out". Flagged for review.)_ |
| 27 | The slothful [man] roasteth not that which he took in hunting: but the substance of a diligent man [is] precious. | The lazy do not roast their game, but the wealth of the diligent is precious. _(correction: The Hebrew verb in the first line is uncertain (roast / flush out). Flagged for review.)_ |
| 28 | In the way of righteousness [is] life; and [in] the pathway [thereof there is] no death. | In the path of righteousness there is life, and in its pathway there is no death. |
