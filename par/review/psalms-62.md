| 1 | Truly my soul waiteth upon God: from him [cometh] my salvation. | Truly my soul waits for God; my salvation comes from him. |

| 4 | They only consult to cast [him] down from his excellency: they delight in lies: they bless with their mouth, but they curse inwardly. Selah. | They only plot to bring him down from his high position; they delight in lies. They bless with their mouths, but curse inwardly. Selah. |
| 5 | My soul, wait thou only upon God; for my expectation [is] from him. | My soul, wait only for God; my hope comes from him. |
| 6 | He only [is] my rock and my salvation: [he is] my defence; I shall not be moved. | He alone is my rock and my salvation, my fortress; I will not be shaken. |

| 8 | Trust in him at all times; [ye] people, pour out your heart before him: God [is] a refuge for us. Selah. | Trust in him at all times, you people; pour out your heart before him. God is a refuge for us. Selah. |
| 9 | Surely men of low degree [are] vanity, [and] men of high degree [are] a lie: to be laid in the balance, they [are] altogether [lighter] than vanity. | Surely people of low standing are a breath, and people of high standing are a lie; if weighed on the scales, together they are lighter than a breath. _(correction: "vanity" became "a breath" (Hebrew hebel: breath, vapor, fleeting nothingness); to a modern reader "vanity" wrongly suggests conceit, not emptiness.)_ |

| 11 | God hath spoken once; twice have I heard this; that power [belongeth] unto God. | God has spoken once; I have heard it twice: that power belongs to God. |
| 12 | Also unto thee, O Lord, [belongeth] mercy: for thou renderest to every man according to his work. | And to you, O Lord, belongs steadfast love; for you repay everyone according to what they have done. _(correction: "mercy" became "steadfast love" (Hebrew chesed: covenant loyalty, unfailing love) -- a different word from, and often confused with, the Hebrew usually rendered "mercy" (rachamim).)_ |
