## Checks, round 1

- **review** (groq): fail
  - v2: The phrase "He only is my rock..." misinterprets the KJV "He only [is] my rock..." which means "He alone is my rock..." The word "only" is used incorrectly as an adverb. -> Change to "He alone is my rock and my salvation; he is my defense; I shall not be greatly moved."
  - v6: Same error as verse 2: "He only is my rock..." should convey exclusivity, not an adverbial "only". -> Change to "He alone is my rock and my salvation; he is my defense; I shall not be moved."
  - v12: Awkward word order "belongs mercy" is not idiomatic modern English and sounds archaic. -> Reorder to "mercy belongs to you, O Lord; for you render to every person according to his work."
